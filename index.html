How we handle your data

  • A written data-processing agreement before any data is shared.
  • De-identified or synthetic data wherever possible, and role-based access for the rest.
  • Documented retention and deletion at the end of each engagement.
  • Processing aligned with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, including cross-border transfer requirements.
  • Every reported figure traceable to its source data and validation rules.
